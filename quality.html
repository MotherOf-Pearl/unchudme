Quality

How our peptides are tested

Our peptides are tested by independent analytical labs. Every report gives purity, identity and the measured amount of peptide in the vial; some add more, as listed below.

What the labs test

PurityHPLC (high-performance liquid chromatography) separates the sample into its components and a UV detector reads each one. Purity is the main peak's share of everything detected, by peak area.
IdentityMass spectrometry weighs the molecule; the ions detected must match the compound's known molecular weight. In a blind test the lab is not told what the sample is and identifies it independently.
ContentThe amount of peptide actually measured in the vial, in mg, against the amount on the label.
AppearanceA visual check of the lyophilized powder (on some reports).
