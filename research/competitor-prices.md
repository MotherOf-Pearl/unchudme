## Caveats

- Many vendors show permanent-looking 'sale' prices (Enhanced Chems, Prime Lab, ezPeps, Artemyx: nearly everything); the displayed sale price is recorded, regular price in the JSON note.
- IDUN and BIOLENO are low-price outliers (e.g. IDUN GLP1-R 30mg $79.99, BIOLENO KLOW $55); they pull means down.
- ezPeps sells overfilled odd sizes (11, 22, 24, 33, 45, 57 mg...) which are in the JSON but don't count toward standard-size stats.
- Ambiguous blends recorded as '10 total' / '5 total' (split not stated) are excluded from the stats.
- Alpha Peps SLU-PP-332 and 5-Amino-1MQ capsule products are capsules, not vials.
- BIOLENO BL-TR2 / BL-RT3 are assumed GLP1-T / GLP1-R from the house codes.
